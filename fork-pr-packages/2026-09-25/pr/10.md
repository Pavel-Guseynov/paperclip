## Thinking Path

> - Paperclip is the open source app people use to manage AI agents for work
> - When the native runtime binds a reviewer, Paperclip creates a completion review for the issue
> - Nothing records which revision and which acceptance contract a review covered. A repeated launch, a new head, or a changed contract cannot be told apart, and review decisions are not tied to a revision (#11390, #10550)
> - This pull request records a review admission in the same transaction that binds the reviewer. The identity is company, issue, exact source SHA, a digest of the acceptance-relevant contract, and a round. The admission's decision is recorded when the review card is resolved
> - The benefit is a durable, deduplicated record of which reviews were admitted for which revision and how they were decided. Later tooling can use it without guessing

## What Changed

- `packages/db`: new `review_admissions` table (migration `0285_massive_wonder_man`, generated with `pnpm --filter @paperclipai/db generate`). It has a composite foreign key to `issues(company_id, id)` and a unique identity on `(company_id, issue_id, source_sha, policy_digest, round)`.
- `packages/shared`: `ReviewAdmission` types and `REVIEW_ADMISSION_DECISIONS` (`approved`, `changes_requested`).
- `server/src/services/review-admission.ts` (new):
  - `admitReview` reads only server-held state:
    - the source SHA from the issue's `commit` work product
    - the contract from the issue
    - the policy digest over the acceptance-relevant execution policy, without `monitor` or other operational fields
    - pull request details from the delivery binding, with no outbound request
  - An active admission deduplicates. A decided revision opens the next round, which supersedes the one before it.
  - The unique index decides races (`onConflictDoNothing` and a re-read).
  - `recordReviewDecision` is company-scoped, and a decision is immutable.
- `server/src/services/native-runtime/status-decision-committer.ts`: the `bind_reviewer` effect admits the review inside a savepoint. A recording failure rolls back only the admission, is logged, and never keeps the issue out of review. An issue with no recorded reviewed head records nothing and launches as before.
- `server/src/services/issue-thread-interactions.ts`: resolving the review card records `approved` or `changes_requested` through `recordReviewDecision`, scoped by company and interaction.
- `doc/execution-semantics.md` §15 (Review Admissions).
- Tests (embedded Postgres; the native status corpus drives the real `commitNativeStatusDecision` path):
  - concurrent admission
  - a unique-index violation
  - cross-company isolation
  - the digest is stable across a monitor write
  - a new round after a decision
  - immutable decisions
  - a database failure inside the savepoint that still commits the review launch

## Risks

- This is a core feature. Nothing reads `review_admissions` yet. It records admissions and decisions, and no behavior depends on it. The compact review context and the typed PR-read or review-submit tools from #11390 are not part of this pull request.
- The admission write adds one savepoint to the reviewer-binding transaction.
- The migration adds a new, empty table.
